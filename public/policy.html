Privacy Policy

WhatsApp Suite by AcquiHire Tech · Effective 23 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

AcquiHire Tech operates WhatsApp Suite at wa.acquihiretech.com. For your own account details we are the data controller. For the contacts and messages you load into the Service, you are the controller and we process that data on your behalf, under your instructions.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, company, time zone, optional profile photo, and a hashed password (we never store your password in readable form).
  • WhatsApp connection details: for each number you connect, its display name, number, WhatsApp Business Account ID, Phone number ID and access token. Access tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Your contacts and content: contact names, phone numbers, emails, tags and notes; message templates; broadcasts; and the messages sent and received through your numbers, with their delivery status and consent or opt-out status.
  • Security and usage records: sign-in and account-action logs, and technical data such as IP address and browser type.
  • Verification codes: one-time codes we send to your email address and to your phone over WhatsApp when you register.

3. How we use it

  • to provide the Service: sign you in, verify your email and phone, send and receive messages through your numbers, and show your dashboard, reports and spend estimates;
  • to keep the Service secure and prevent abuse;
  • to respect opt-outs: if a contact replies with an opt-out keyword, we record it and stop messaging them in the affected categories;
  • to measure how people find and sign up for the Service, as described under "Analytics" below.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use your contacts' data for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp): messages, recipient numbers and related details are sent to and received from the WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta's own policies apply to that data.
  • Email delivery provider: to send verification and password-reset emails.
  • Hosting provider: our servers and database are hosted by a third-party host.
  • Google and Meta analytics: see below.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5. Analytics

On the sign-in, registration and verification pages only, we use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to count visits and sign-ups and to measure our own advertising. These tools set cookies or similar identifiers and receive page paths, never the content of your account or any contacts or messages. We do not run them inside the dashboard. You can block these cookies in your browser settings.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal details are removed or anonymised and your connected WhatsApp accounts, contacts and messages are deleted from our database. Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle. We may keep limited records where the law requires.

7. Security

Passwords are hashed, WhatsApp access tokens are encrypted, connections use HTTPS, and webhook requests from Meta are verified by signature. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us at once if you suspect misuse.

8. Your rights and deleting your data

You can view and edit your details on your profile page and delete your account there; deletion asks for your password and removes your data as described above. You can also email info@acquihiretech.com to ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your data, and we will respond within a reasonable time (and within the period the law requires). If you are a contact of one of our customers, please make requests to that business first, since they control your data; we will help them act on it.

9. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18.

10. Changes and contact

We will change the effective date above when we update this policy. Questions or complaints: info@acquihiretech.com.
